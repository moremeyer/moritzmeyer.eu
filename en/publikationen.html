Master’s thesis · University of Münster · 2026

Artificial Intelligence and Employment in Germany: A Task-Based Analysis of Exposure, Substitution, and Complementarity

How is artificial intelligence changing employment in Germany? The thesis rates every officially defined task in the German Classification of Occupations by how strongly AI affects, substitutes or complements it, and links the results to employment data. The finding: AI is currently reshaping jobs mainly by changing the tasks within them rather than causing uniform job losses.
